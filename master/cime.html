Physical Constants

E3SM takes its physical constants from the Physical Constants Dictionary (PCD), a YAML file found at share/pcd.yaml in E3SM. MPAS-Tools packages a copy of this file in mpas_tools.constants. During tests of the rattler-build package build, the copy is checked against the one on E3SM’s master branch.

PCD constants

Use mpas_tools.constants.get_constant() to get a constant by its PCD name:

from mpas_tools.constants import get_constant

earth_radius = get_constant('mean_radius')

Some of the constants are given by a formula in terms of other constants, rather than a value. These are evaluated in double precision, in the same way as in the compiled models. mpas_tools.constants.get_pcd_version() gives the version of the packaged PCD.

CIME constants

The dictionary constants in mpas_tools.cime.constants contains constants with the names and values of those in E3SM’s share/util/shr_const_mod.F90. E3SM sets most of these from the PCD, and MPAS-Tools does the same. A few, such as SHR_CONST_SDAY and SHR_CONST_KARMAN, are still set in shr_const_mod itself. The constants that shr_const_mod derives from other constants (e.g. SHR_CONST_RGAS) are not included. During tests of the rattler-build package build, each constant is checked against shr_const_mod on E3SM’s master branch.

Some of the constants most likely to be useful in MPAS-Tools, Polaris and other related projects are (with their PCD names):

  • SHR_CONST_CDAY - sec in calendar day (s), seconds_per_day

  • SHR_CONST_REARTH - radius of Earth (m), mean_radius

  • SHR_CONST_G - acceleration of gravity (m/s^2), standard_acceleration_of_gravity

  • SHR_CONST_RHOFW - density of fresh water (kg/m^3), pure_water_density_reference

  • SHR_CONST_RHOSW - density of sea water (kg/m^3), seawater_density_reference

  • SHR_CONST_RHOICE - density of ice (kg/m^3), sea_ice_density_reference

  • SHR_CONST_CPFW - specific heat of fresh water (J/kg/K), pure_water_specific_heat_capacity_reference

  • SHR_CONST_CPSW - specific heat of sea water (J/kg/K), seawater_specific_heat_capacity_reference

  • SHR_CONST_CPICE - specific heat of fresh ice (J/kg/K), sea_ice_specific_heat_capacity_reference

  • SHR_CONST_LATICE - latent heat of fusion (J/kg), latent_heat_of_fusion_reference

  • SHR_CONST_LATVAP - latent heat of evaporation (J/kg), latent_heat_of_vaporization_reference

Updating the PCD

When E3SM updates its PCD, copy E3SM’s share/pcd.yaml to conda_package/mpas_tools/constants/pcd.yaml. If E3SM has also changed which PCD constants shr_const_mod.F90 uses, update conda_package/mpas_tools/cime/constants.py to match.
